Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

HAS-KESK OU ("we", "our", or "us") is responsible for the personal information described in this policy. This policy explains how we handle information in DailyCardJoy, also displayed as Daily Card Joy (the "Game"), including its online services.

This policy explains our practices; it does not replace any permission or consent required for a particular use of your information.

At a glance: Puzzle progress and settings are saved on your device. The Game also connects to our services and uses Adjust to analyze our own app and links. Installation identifiers, usage information, and diagnostics can therefore be processed outside your device. The iOS Game does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA) or request App Tracking Transparency permission. You can contact us about your data.

1. Information We Process

Local puzzle progress and preferences

The puzzle mode stores completed levels, the current board, unlocked rewards, tutorial progress, and music or sound preferences locally on your device. This local save feature does not provide account-based cloud synchronization. It is separate from the network and measurement data described below.

Device and service information

The Game does not offer account registration, login, purchases, top-ups, or chat, and does not use location data.

The Game creates an app-specific installation identifier and sends it with the game identifier to our configuration service. It also reports the outcome of startup access checks, which can include a detected VPN or network-tunnel condition. We use this information to deliver configuration, determine service availability, and investigate access problems.

When your device connects to our services or measurement providers, they receive network and technical information, such as your IP address, request time, device or operating-system information, and app or SDK version. This information is used to operate the services and measure their use.

Installation, usage, and attribution information

When Adjust is active, it processes installation and session activity, permitted device identifiers, and technical metadata. These identifiers can include the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and an Adjust identifier. IDFA reading and use of Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework are disabled in our iOS integration.

We use attribution information to understand activity from our own links and app. Measurement data can be associated with an installation or device identifier even though the Game has no account registration. See Section 3 for the limits on this use.

Information you send to us

If you contact us for support or make a privacy request, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to provide. Please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or other information that is not needed for your request.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide the Game, restore local puzzle progress, and remember your preferences.
  • Deliver configuration and online services, evaluate access conditions, and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • Understand installations and sessions, measure how people use the Game, and improve its reliability and experience.
  • Understand how our own links lead to installations and use of the Game.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests, protect our services, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on the basis appropriate to the activity: providing a service you request, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Game, legal obligations, or your consent where required.

3. Adjust and First-Party Analytics

We integrate Adjust, provided by Adjust GmbH, to analyze our own app and links. Adjust receives the measurement data described in Section 1 and provides installation-source and usage reporting on our behalf.

We do not send device data to advertising platforms or link data from the Game with data from other companies' apps or websites for advertising or advertising attribution. Our use of Adjust is limited to first-party analytics.

The iOS Game does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission and does not read or send the IDFA, including if tracking permission was previously granted. Configuration requests and first-party analytics still process installation identifiers, session activity, technical metadata, and diagnostics as described in this policy. Removing an ATT prompt does not mean that no data is collected.

For details about Adjust's processing and available privacy options, see the Adjust Privacy Policy.

4. When Information Is Shared

  • Service providers: Providers supporting hosting, configuration, support communications, and analytics process information needed to provide those services. Adjust is described above.
  • Legal and safety purposes: Information may be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect users, investigate abuse, or establish or defend legal claims.

We require service providers handling information on our behalf to protect it, use it only for the authorized purposes, and provide protection consistent with this policy and applicable requirements.

Apple separately processes information for App Store or TestFlight distribution and related platform services under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Local Storage and Online Services

The Game uses local storage for puzzle saves, settings, cached configuration, and attribution state. Online services accessed inside the Game may also use cookies or similar storage to maintain service state and preferences. Information processed through these services is covered by this policy; storing puzzle progress locally does not mean that the entire Game operates without data collection.

If you follow a link to an independently operated website or service, its privacy notice explains its own processing. This does not remove our responsibility for information processed through the Game.

6. Retention and Deletion

Local puzzle saves and preferences remain on your device until the associated app data is removed. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data; offloading it may preserve that data, and device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Server-side configuration, access, measurement, and support records are retained only for as long as needed for their stated purposes. Retention depends on the type of record, the period needed to analyze app usage or resolve a support issue, and any legal or security requirements. Records should be deleted or anonymized when those purposes no longer require identifiable data.

Uninstalling the Game does not automatically delete information already held by us or our providers. To request deletion, email eenokk@haskesk.homes. We will review your request, identify the relevant records, and coordinate with providers where necessary. If some information must be retained by law, we will explain the applicable limitation.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; restrict or object to certain processing; withdraw consent; and complain to your local data-protection authority.

  • Analytics and diagnostics: Contact us about processing of your installation, usage, or diagnostic records. The Game does not use ATT permission as a control for these first-party services.
  • Local saves: Remove the app and its data to remove local progress, taking any device backups into account.
  • Access, deletion, or other privacy requests: Use the contact email below and describe what you would like us to do.

For a request, identify the Game as DailyCardJoy and provide enough information to help us locate the relevant records, such as the approximate dates you used it and your device model. We may request additional information reasonably needed to verify or fulfill your request. We do not require you to create a game account to contact us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

We and our providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law restricts an international transfer, appropriate safeguards are required, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual protections. You can contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your data.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information without the consent required by applicable law, please contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate steps, including deleting the information where required.
